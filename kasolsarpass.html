Kasol & Sar Pass Trek

The Mini Israel of India

Kasol's unique hippie culture blends Indian and Israeli influences. The main street is lined with cafes serving authentic Middle Eastern food, and the tranquil Parvati River flows alongside.

Sar Pass Summit (13,800 ft)

The Sar Pass Trek is a captivating high-altitude adventure in the Parvati Valley of Himachal Pradesh, India, culminating at the Sar Pass summit at 13,800 feet (4,205 meters). It's renowned for its diverse landscapes, ranging from dense forests and alpine meadows to snow-covered trails, making it a favorite among both novice and seasoned trekkers.​

Best Season: May-June & Sept-Oct
Difficulty: Moderate to Challenging

Tosh Valley & Waterfalls

Tosh Valley, located in Himachal Pradesh's Parvati Valley at around 2,400 meters altitude, is a serene Himalayan village known for its scenic beauty, peaceful vibe, and trekking opportunities. A short hike from the village leads to the picturesque Tosh Waterfall, a tranquil spot surrounded by lush greenery and ideal for nature lovers. The valley also offers stunning views of snow-capped peaks, charming wooden houses, apple orchards, and access to nearby treks like Kheerganga and Kutla. With cozy cafés, local Himachali culture, and the soothing sound of the Parvati River, Tosh is a perfect offbeat getaway for trekkers and peace-seekers alike.
