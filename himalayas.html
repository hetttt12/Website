Himalayas

Tirthan Valley

Nestled at the edge of the Great Himalayan National Park, Tirthan Valley is a tranquil haven known for trout fishing, river crossings, and trekking. Its lush greenery and snow-capped mountain views make it ideal for nature enthusiasts seeking serenity away from commercialization.

Nar Phu Valley

A hidden gem in the Annapurna region, the Nar Phu Valley trek takes you through ancient Tibetan villages, rugged landscapes, and high mountain passes. The journey offers a blend of adventure, culture, and untouched natural beauty.​

Best Season: May-June & Sept-Oct
Difficulty: Moderate to Challenging
View from Sar Pass

Lower Dolpo and Lake Phoksumdo

This remote trek traverses traditional villages and valleys, offering stunning views of snow-capped peaks and the pristine Phoksumdo Lake. The region is rich in ancient Bon-Po culture and provides a glimpse into Nepal's wild-west traditions.
