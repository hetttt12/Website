Lama Dugh

​Lama Dugh is a serene alpine meadow located near Manali, Himachal Pradesh, at an elevation of approximately 9,900 feet (3,018 meters). Accessible via a moderate trek starting from the Manu Temple in Old Manali, the trail spans about 12 to 17 kilometers round trip, depending on the chosen route, and typically takes 6 to 8 hours to complete. The path meanders through dense forests of deodar, pine, and oak, gradually opening up to expansive meadows adorned with vibrant alpine flowers. Trekkers are rewarded with panoramic views of majestic peaks such as Deo Tibba, Indrasan, and the Bara Shigri Glacier, making it a perfect day hike for nature enthusiasts and beginners alike.

Shoja

Shoja (Shozha) is a peaceful, offbeat village in Himachal Pradesh's Seraj Valley, located around 5 km from Jalori Pass at an altitude of 2,630 meters. Known for its untouched natural beauty, it offers panoramic mountain views, dense forests, and easy access to scenic spots like Serolsar Lake and Raghupur Fort. Ideal for trekking, birdwatching, and solitude, Shoja is best visited between April and June for pleasant weather and lush landscapes.​

Best Season: May-June & Sept-Oct
Difficulty: Moderate to Challenging

Rasol

Rasol is a remote Himalayan village perched above Kasol in the Parvati Valley of Himachal Pradesh, accessible only by a steep 3–4 hour trek from Chalal village. Situated at around 3,000 meters, it remains largely untouched by commercialization, offering peace, panoramic mountain views, and traditional wooden homes. Known for its simplicity and spiritual atmosphere, Rasol is ideal for those seeking solitude, nature, and a break from tourist crowds. It’s best visited between April and October, when the weather is pleasant and trails are accessible.
